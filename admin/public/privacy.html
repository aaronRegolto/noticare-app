Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 4, 2026

In short: NotiCare checks your text messages for scams on your phone. Your messages, sender numbers, and links stay on your phone. We only receive anonymous scan results, and the text of a message only when you choose Block & Report on it.

About NotiCare

NotiCare is an Android app that helps protect you from SMS phishing ("smishing"). It checks new text messages and links you open, and warns you when something looks like a scam. This policy explains what data the app uses, what it sends to our servers, and the choices you have.

What stays on your phone

  • The full text of your SMS messages, and who sent them.
  • Links inside messages and links you open through Safe Link Protection.
  • Your message History, which is stored encrypted on the phone.
  • Clipboard contents you paste into Manual Scan.

The scam check itself runs entirely on your phone. NotiCare never opens or loads a link to check it.

What we collect

Data Why
Your phone number To sign you in with a one-time SMS code. Our database keeps only the last four digits for display; the full number is held by our sign-in provider and passed to our SMS provider to deliver the code.
Scan results: threat level, a rounded score (always 0 for a Safe result), warning-sign codes (for example "shortened link"), the number of links, whether the message was blocked, and the time To show community statistics on the Dashboard and improve detection. These never include message text, senders, or links.
A random installation ID, the app version, and the detection rule version To keep uploads reliable and know which detection rules produced a result. It is not a hardware or advertising ID.
The text of a message you choose to Block & Report, with its risk score To warn other NotiCare users about the same scam in the "Most Reported Phishing Messages" list. This is the only time message text leaves your phone, and only when you tap Block & Report.
Your feedback on a scan result ("this should have been Safe", for example) To find and fix detection mistakes.
Your plan (Free or Pro) and its expiry date To unlock Pro features. Payments are handled by PayMongo; we never see or store your card or wallet details.

We do not collect your contacts, location, photos, or advertising ID. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

Android permissions NotiCare uses

  • Notification access: to read new notifications from your default Messages app only, so new texts can be checked as they arrive. Notifications from every other app are ignored without being read.
  • Notifications: to warn you about risky messages.
  • Display over other apps and full-screen notifications (optional): to show a full-screen scam warning on top of the app you are using, or on the lock screen.
  • Default browser (optional): so Safe Link Protection can check links before they open. Safe links are passed straight to the browser you choose.
  • Internet: to sign in and to upload the anonymous results described above.

NotiCare does not request permission to read, receive, or send SMS messages.

Who we share data with

  • Supabase, which hosts our database and phone sign-in.
  • Semaphore, which delivers the one-time sign-in code by SMS. It receives your phone number and the code, and nothing else.
  • PayMongo, which processes Pro payments.
  • Other NotiCare users see the text of messages reported with Block & Report, without any information about who reported them.

We may also disclose data if required by law.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). History on your phone is encrypted and tied to your account, so another account on the same phone cannot read it. Access to our database is restricted to your own records and to a small number of administrators.

How long we keep data

  • Account data and scan results: until you delete your account.
  • Reported scam messages and anonymous community totals: kept after account deletion, because they are not linked to you.
  • History on your phone: until you clear it, sign out and delete it, or uninstall the app.

Your choices

  • Turn Real-Time Scanning or Safe Link Protection off at any time.
  • Delete single messages or clear your whole History in the app.
  • Choose Ignore instead of Block & Report, so nothing is shared.
  • Delete your account in the app under Settings & Privacy > Delete account. See how to delete your account for other options.

Children

NotiCare is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and, for important changes, let you know in the app.
