Delete your NotiCare account

NotiCare for Android

Delete it in the app

  1. Open NotiCare and sign in.
  2. On the Dashboard, open Settings & Privacy.
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete account.
  4. Confirm twice. Your account is deleted right away and you are signed out.

Can't use the app?

If you uninstalled NotiCare or can no longer sign in, email support@noticare.site from any address with the subject "Delete my NotiCare account" and the phone number you signed in with. We will confirm the request with a one-time code sent to that number, then delete the account within 30 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your account and the phone numbers linked to it.
  • Your scan results, feedback, and registered devices.
  • Your plan (Free or Pro) and its payment link. Any remaining Pro time is not refunded.
  • When deleted from the app: your message History on that phone.

What is kept

  • The text of scam messages you reported with Block & Report stays in the community list, so others keep being warned. It is not linked to you after deletion.
  • Anonymous community totals (for example, "12 dangerous messages today"), which never identified you.
  • Payment records PayMongo must keep by law.
