Privacy, plainly stated

Your messages are
yours.

Manual scans are analyzed on your device. Photos and full message text are not uploaded during a scan. If you choose Report, that specific message is shared to help warn the community. Real-time scanning checks notifications from your default Messages app only, when you turn it on.

NotiCare does not read your SMS inbox or delete SMS messages. If Android allows a dangerous notification to be suppressed, the original SMS still remains in Messages.
