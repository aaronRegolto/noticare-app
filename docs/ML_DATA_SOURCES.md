# Scam and phishing data sources

This is a source shortlist, not an assertion that every source is licensed for inclusion in a commercial app. Recheck each source's current terms, provenance, privacy requirements, and update frequency before collecting or redistributing any data.

## Used in the current SMS model

| Source                                                                                 | What it contains                                                          | Use and limitations                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| [UCI SMS Spam Collection](https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/228/sms+spam+collection) | SMS labeled ham/spam.                                                     | General English-heavy spam corpus, not a Philippine phishing dataset. UCI describes source material from a UK spam forum, Singapore research volunteers, and other academic corpora. The `ham` label is not a guarantee that a message is safe.                                                       |
| NCSU SMS phishing CSV in `ml/datasets/`                                                | Reported phishing message examples used by the current training manifest. | The checked-in file has no label column; the manifest treats its rows as phishing and caps the source. Confirm its originating terms and provenance before redistribution.                                                                                                                            |
| User-provided `text-messages.csv`                                                      | Categorized messages including the `spam` label.                          | The importer uses only `category=spam`, strips sender/date columns, and masks common identifiers. It does not use unlabeled `SPAM_SMS.csv` rows. The sanitized 725 unique messages are included in model 3.2.0. This file is local user-provided data, not a public dataset or independent benchmark. |

The current dataset is not enough to claim production-grade Philippine performance. It has no reviewed local legitimate-message complement and no independent real-world Filipino phishing holdout.

## Public URL intelligence feeds

These are useful for link-checker test/evaluation pipelines, not for SMS text-model training. Keep URLs inert: never browse to a listed URL as part of dataset processing.

| Source                                                                                                                   | Feed characteristics                                                                                                                                                                       | Important limitations                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| [PhishTank developer information](https://phishtank.org/developer_info.php) and [terms](https://phishtank.org/terms.php) | Downloadable verified phishing URL data; CSV/JSON/XML fields include URL, verification/online status, target brand, and timestamps. An application key is required for repeated downloads. | Terms can change and current Cisco terms apply. Feed records do not generally say whether a URL was seen on Facebook, Messenger, or another platform. Obtain legal approval before product use or redistribution.                         |
| [OpenPhish feeds](https://openphish.com/phishing_feeds.html) and [terms](https://openphish.com/terms.html)               | Community phishing URL feed; paid intelligence packs add metadata such as target, geography, and page language.                                                                            | The current Community Feed terms restrict use to personal, academic, independent, or personal research; use for a product, threat detection, or customer protection requires prior written permission. It has not been added to NotiCare. |
| [URLhaus API](https://urlhaus.abuse.ch/api/)                                                                             | Community feeds of malware-distribution URLs and associated metadata.                                                                                                                      | Malware URL intelligence is not the same label as phishing. Downloads require an Auth-Key; commercial/for-profit use may require the paid service under its fair-use terms. It is not included in SMS training.                           |

Public feeds are time-sensitive threat intelligence, not immutable ground truth. Use time-stamped snapshots, deduplicate by campaign/domain, preserve source attribution, record terms, and test on later time windows. Avoid embedding live malicious URLs in documentation or opening them during evaluation.

## Philippine data leads and collection plan

- The supplied `text-messages.csv` is the only Philippine-labeled spam source used in the current candidate. Its spam-only labels do not provide local benign examples.
- The [National Privacy Commission](https://privacy.gov.ph/) explains the Philippine Data Privacy Act and is a privacy/compliance resource, not a public message dataset.
- The [Securities and Exchange Commission](https://www.sec.gov.ph/) and [Cybercrime Investigation and Coordinating Center](https://cicc.gov.ph/) publish advisories and reporting channels that can help define scam categories and annotation guidance. Treat advisories as taxonomy/reference material, not labeled SMS training examples.
- No open, consent-cleared, representative Philippine spam/phishing/online-lending harassment message corpus was identified for this update. Complaints and loan-collection messages can expose highly sensitive personal data; do not scrape or train on them without an appropriate lawful basis, explicit notice/consent where required, minimization, access controls, and review by Philippine privacy counsel.

For a useful local corpus, invite volunteers to submit messages specifically for model improvement, redact identifiers on-device, obtain separate training consent, label scam family and threat severity with two reviewers, keep legitimate Filipino/Taglish messages, and hold out later campaigns/time periods. Store raw reports separately from training data and document removal requests.

## Social-media-specific data gap

PhishTank, OpenPhish, and URLhaus identify URLs or hosts, but their public feed fields generally do not record the social app or in-app browser where a user encountered a link. They therefore cannot honestly be described as “Facebook phishing URLs” or “Messenger phishing URLs” without additional source evidence.

NotiCare checks a link shared explicitly to the app, or links that Android routes through NotiCare as the selected default browser. Once checked, it offers installed browsers for the user to choose. It cannot force Facebook/Messenger to hand off taps kept inside their WebViews: Android link intents only apply when the source app dispatches the URL outside its own browser. TLS and a local VPN do not provide a reliable way to recover the full URL or show a chooser for those internal taps. Do not claim VPN interception. Collect any future platform attribution only through a voluntary, privacy-reviewed report flow and keep the platform label separate from the URL reputation label.
